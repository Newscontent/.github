# Code of Conduct

EyeNewz is a professional news product for India. Everyone in the Newscontent org is expected to:

- Be respectful in issues, PRs, and reviews
- Stay in the repo that matches your role
- Not share production secrets, user data, or unpublished stories
- Assume good intent; disagree on the work, not the person

Report conduct concerns to **support@eyenewz.com** or an org owner.
